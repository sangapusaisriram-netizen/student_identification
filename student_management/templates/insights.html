TRANSPARENT MODEL

How the indicators are calculated

These are intervention signals, not a predictive model or a final academic judgement. Subject-by-subject attendance is optional; only recorded classes contribute.

01

Success score

Academic performance 30%, overall recorded attendance 20%, assignment completion 10%, LMS login frequency 5%, engagement 10%, placement assessments 10%, assessed skills 10%, and feedback 5%. Subject-wise attendance is optional. Missing measures are excluded and remaining weights are normalized.

02

Placement readiness

Aptitude, coding, and mock interviews 45%, assessed skills 35%, and application progress 20%. Application stages rise from Applied to Selected.

03

Risk flag

Risk contributors include backlogs, marks, attendance, LMS activity, engagement, skill/placement assessments, and student/faculty feedback. Missing evidence is flagged separately, never scored as failure.
